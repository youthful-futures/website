Privacy notice

Last updated 24 October 2026

This notice explains what personal data Youthful Futures handles through this website (youthfulfutures.org and youthfulfutures.com) and when you write to us, why we handle it, and the choices and rights you have.

Who we are

Youthful Futures is responsible for the personal data described in this notice. You can reach us about anything in it at contact@youthfulfutures.org.

The short version

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, social media plug-ins or embedded third-party content.
  • All fonts and images are served from our own site. Your browser does not contact any other service when you visit.
  • There are no forms. We only receive personal data if you choose to email us.
  • We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

When you visit the website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. When you load a page, GitHub’s servers automatically record technical information such as your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser type. GitHub uses this to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not have access to these logs and do not use them to identify visitors.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website. GitHub may process this data in the United States. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you choose to share in your message. We use this only to read and reply to you, and to continue a conversation you have started, for example about a partnership.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps you have asked for before entering into an agreement. Our email is provided by Google Workspace, which processes messages on our behalf.

We keep correspondence only for as long as we need it for the conversation and any follow-up, then delete it. Please do not send sensitive information, such as health details, by email.

Young people

This website is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are under 16, please ask a parent, guardian or teacher before writing to us. If you think a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the service providers named above (GitHub for hosting and Google for email), who act on our instructions, or where the law requires it. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. These can include the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use it
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it
  • complain to your local data protection authority

To use any of these rights, email contact@youthfulfutures.org. We will reply within one month.

Changes to this notice

If we add features that handle personal data in new ways, such as a newsletter sign-up, we will update this notice before they go live and change the date at the top.
