01 / Purpose

A world in which a young person’s circumstances do not determine their ability to learn, choose and create a livelihood.

Youthful Futures is a global initiative for underserved young people. Our goal is young people who can learn something unfamiliar, make something useful and act on opportunities with confidence.

02 / Focus

Capability that keeps growing

01

Independent learning

Finding a starting point, asking useful questions and learning what a new task requires.

02

Agency

Making informed choices and seeing an idea through to something useful.

03

Resilient livelihoods

Recognising opportunities and adapting as work and circumstances change.

These are the initiative’s intended outcomes. Programme results will be measured and reported separately.

Our approach: learn, make, act

Learn

Learn something unfamiliar.

Find a starting point, ask questions and keep going. The ability to learn a new thing is the skill that lasts.

Make

Make something useful.

Try an idea, put it in front of people and use the feedback. Practical work connects learning to real needs.

Act

Act on opportunity. With confidence.

Recognise a possibility, choose a next step and adapt as circumstances change.

03 / Learning through setbacks

Stronger from setbacks, not just surviving them.

Our ambition is for young people to become anti-fragile. A first attempt can teach us something. A setback can change the next question we ask. With reflection, feedback and support, young people can build the confidence to try again and the capability to choose a new approach.

  1. Try
  2. Reflect
  3. Adapt
  4. Choose a next step
  5. Ask for support

04 / AI & learning

AI is a tool. Young people set the direction.

As work changes, the people who thrive will be those who can keep learning. We are shaping learning experiences around real tasks and the priorities of young people, using technology, including AI where useful, to develop lasting capability.

Learner-led

We start with the learner’s agency. What do they want to understand? What could they make? What could they act on next?

Capability first

Tools serve the goal of learning to learn. The aim is judgement and confidence, not dependence on any single technology.

Grounded in real lives

Learning connects to local priorities, real tasks and the routes to opportunity that matter where young people live.
