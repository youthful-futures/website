A young person’s circumstances should not decide their future.

Youthful Futures is a global initiative for underserved young people, centred on independent learning, agency and resilient livelihoods, so that every young person has the confidence and capability to shape what comes next.

About Youthful Futures

The world of work is being rewritten. Young people should have a hand in writing it.

AI and new technologies are changing what work looks like, what skills matter and how people learn. For young people with limited access to opportunity, there is a real risk of being left out of a transition they did not choose.

There is also a real opening. Learning has never been more within reach, if young people have the confidence to take on something unfamiliar, the agency to make their own choices, and the support to grow stronger from setbacks rather than simply survive them.

Youthful Futures is being built to help make that opening real: alongside young people, the people who work with them, and partners who share the ambition.

Our vision

A world in which every young person can learn, choose and create a livelihood, wherever they start.

People

Shaped by the people in it.

Youthful Futures is a shared effort. Young people set the direction, and a community of educators, artists, practitioners and advisors helps create the conditions for them to learn, make and act.

Our work

Three ideas guide everything we build.

Independent learning

The confidence to take on something unfamiliar, and the habit of learning what a new task requires.

Agency

Making informed choices and seeing an idea through to something useful.

Resilient livelihoods

Recognising opportunity and adapting as work and circumstances change, with AI as a tool, not the goal.
